# Evidence Fusion Skill

Purpose: turn independent search-agent findings into ranked, auditable evidence clusters.

## Procedure
1. Normalize URLs, media hashes, timestamps and source identities.
2. Cluster exact and near-duplicate media.
3. Separate independent domains/providers from repost chains.
4. Compare face, visual, text, temporal and source-quality signals.
5. Build provenance links between originals, derivatives, embeds and reposts.
6. Generate bounded follow-up searches from high-value new entities.
7. Stop recursion when budget is exhausted or new information yield is negligible.

## Confidence
Confidence is evidence aggregation, not identity certainty. A face match must remain a candidate unless independently corroborated.
